# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Rekann contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, and focus discussions on improving the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publication of private information, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. Reports should be sent privately through the repository's security reporting channel when they contain sensitive information. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporters' privacy as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
